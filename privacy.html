Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Türkçe metin aşağıda.

This Privacy Policy describes how Semih Saydam ("we") handles information in the game Afterdrift (the "App").

We Collect No Data

Afterdrift works entirely offline. It does not create accounts, does not connect to our servers or any third-party service, and does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress (unlocked upgrades, a run in progress, achievements, a local log of your runs) and your settings (sound, controls, vibration) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Crash Reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

Children

Because the App collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example Game Center), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
