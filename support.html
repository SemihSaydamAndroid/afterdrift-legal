Afterdrift Support

Afterdrift is an isometric drift-combat roguelike. You are Vale, a racer who crashed at The Last Turn and woke up in The Yard, a junkyard between life and death. Fight through Dust Flats, The Pit and The Last Turn, take boons from three racing ghosts, and face The Collector.

How to play

  • Touch: drag on the left side of the screen to steer. On the right: GAS, BRAKE (above GAS; hold it while stopped to reverse), DRIFT and NITRO. Slide your thumb from DRIFT up to NITRO to boost out of a drift. ROCKET and MINE appear in combat.
  • Your main gun fires on its own at enemies in front of your car: aim with the nose of your car.
  • Drifting fills nitro and weapon energy. The first moments of a drift let bullets pass through you.
  • Game controllers are supported. You can switch the touch layout and turn vibration off in Settings.
  • If you close the game during a run, you can continue from the room you were in.
